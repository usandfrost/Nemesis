The firmware (Drumboy_Pro_Fw_Nemesis_...bin, in the install package) also holds:

- The Drumboy Pro stock firmware, by Randomwaves: the board's start-up, the display's start-up sequence, the audio codec driver and the set-up of the chip's peripherals. MIT licence, below.
