Sending and deleting are not yet proven

Over a real MIDI cable, this page has listed a Drumboy Pro's presets, backed them up and saved a song as MIDI. Sending and deleting presets write to the card, and they have not yet been tried on a real one. Until they have, keep a copy of your card before you send or delete.

You can try everything with the simulated device. It is a program in this page, not a Drumboy Pro, and nothing you do with it reaches a card.

What a real connection will need

The Drumboy Pro has MIDI sockets, and its USB-C socket is for power. A computer reaches it through a USB-MIDI interface wired to both sockets, in and out.

The browser needs Web MIDI with permission for system exclusive messages. Chrome and Edge have it, Firefox needs a permission add-on, and Safari does not have it. The protocol is in docs/PRESET_SYSEX.md.
