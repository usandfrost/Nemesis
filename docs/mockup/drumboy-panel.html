Drumboy Panel Mockup

CPU simulation: Draft interaction design. Turn a knob by dragging it up or down or with the mouse wheel; click the keys. Key functions are provisional until the printed legends are confirmed.

Turn

Drag a knob up or down, or scroll over it. Click a knob and use ↑↓. Hold Shift (or the grid key, bottom right) for coarse steps.

Play and step

  • PLAY view: bottom keys 1-10 pick a lane. SHIFT + T3 PLAYED BY moves the picked lane to another part: A or B (CATHODE synths) or C (the sampler, where the lane plays its own sample and its notes become pitch). Without SHIFT, T3 does nothing, so a stray turn cannot move a lane.
  • Drum lanes: press S again for view 2, SAMPLE PER STEP. Pick a step with ◀ ▶ (or hold its key) and turn B1 STEP SAMPLE: that trig plays its own sample, so one lane can carry kick, rim and clap. It is a sample lock: ○ on a locked step clears it.
  • Patterns: P1-P16. The PATTERN knob (or SHIFT + key 1-10) picks one; while playing it waits for the end of the current pattern. SHIFT + C / SHIFT + P copy and paste a whole pattern, also into another project.
  • S (bottom right) opens SONG: rows of pattern + repeats. + and − pick a row, ● adds one, ○ removes it, T3/T4 set its pattern and repeats, PLAY or OK plays the song from that row. C / P copy and paste a whole song.
  • On every list (presets and samples in BROWSE, song rows, card slots) + − move the selection; in BROWSE ◀ ▶ change the category and OK loads.
  • I opens PROJECT: T1 key, T2 scale; B1 or + − pick a slot on the SD card, OK loads it, SHIFT + OK saves the whole project (patterns, songs, sounds) there. With a scale set, every synth note snaps to it (chords and arpeggios too); drum lanes are not touched.
  • STEP key: bottom keys 1-8 toggle steps, 9 and 10 turn the page. Hold a step key and turn a knob to edit that step without toggling it; hold and release without turning to open its lock.
  • Turning LENGTH or HITS (the Euclid rhythm) rewrites the lane; taps on steps are an overlay on top of it.

P-locks

In STEP view, hold a step key (about a third of a second, or keep a number key down), open any sound page and turn a knob: that value belongs to that step only and shows amber. Tap the same step to leave, ○ clears its locks, tapping another step moves the lock.

Sound and sampler

The 3x3 block on the right opens the sound pages: eight labelled parameters each. D switches part, F opens the browser (presets for a synth part, samples for a sampler part), M the mixer with the cost meter. Part C is a sampler: each of its lanes plays its own sample, with start, end, pitch, reverse, loop and a filter.

Chords and arpeggio

In STEP view press S again for view 2. The lower knobs set the chord, inversion, voicing and strum of the selected step; the upper knobs set the lane's arpeggio (mode, rate per step, octaves, gate). A step with a chord is marked white underneath; an arpeggio plays the chord's notes across the step.

Getting back

L goes back one step, a second L to the main lane screen; SHIFT + L (or H) goes there at once. Esc also goes back one step. The screen is not a touch screen: its corners are labels. Pressing the key of the page you are on, or OK after choosing a sample or preset, also goes back.

Keyboard

Space play and stop · 1-0 bottom keys · arrows move · Enter OK · Backspace clear · S step · L lanes · F browser
