# Fit of the original CATHODE presets to the Drumboy panel

Corpus: 383 presets (`ref/upstream/docs/dev/sound-design/archive/presets/*/`, the T-CAL-55 cut archive; the shipped `Resources/presets/` set is not in the clone, and the brief's 403 is not found: 383 JSON files, one preset each). 8 banks: vintage-poly 106, signature-wave 57, vintage-mono 51, synthpop 47, nordic 41, signature 40, techno 28, signature-pilot 13.

## Rule

Every source parameter is converted to the panel's units through the registry laws (`cathode-registry.tsv`) and classified exact / approximated / dropped. Each carries a loss in points; **score = 100 - sum(loss)**, floor 0. Budgets: oscillator layers 30 (split by audible level, times 1 - fidelity of the layer's mode), filter 8 + filter envelope 4, amp envelope clipping 2.5 each, mod-matrix rows 3.0 x importance(dest) x min(1, depth/32) when dropped (cap 28), FX chain 5-7 per reverb/delay/chorus scaled by mix (cap 22), fold/resonator/FM/punch 3-4 each, per-oscillator envelopes 2.5. Cost: the firmware's own `voiceCost` (via `panel_tool cost`); a preset whose voice is too dear for its category (BASS/LEAD 2 voices, chords 3) is degraded step by step (width, companding, drive, ring, sync, filter 2, heavy filter, unison) and the loss is charged. Two variants: **now** (WAVE is SIN/TRI/SAW/SQR only) and **wt** (a wavetable carries WT-mode oscillators; the table is on the device). Fidelity is 1.0 only where the panel has the same thing; the rest (PD types other than Saw, ALGO engines, Bitmask, Pulse width, Reed/Scream filters, comb/formant) take fixed fidelities in `convert_presets.py`.

## Wavetables

The catalogue has **200 factory banks**, all generated by procedural kernels (no stored data in the upstream tree): Basic 30, Vocal 25, Metallic 35, Harmonic 40, Chip 30, Complex 40. Frames per bank: 16 frames x 17, 32 frames x 157, 64 frames x 26; each frame 2048 samples. All 200 banks as 16-bit PCM: 28.5 MB (6960 frames). Resident in the engine each frame is expanded to a band-limited mip pyramid (11 levels x 2048 floats = 90 KB per frame, 2.9 MB per 32-frame bank), so residency, not storage, is the limit; `FIRMWARE_INTEGRATION.md` proposes decimated mips (about 4.1 k samples per frame instead of 22.5 k).

150 of 383 presets run an audible oscillator on a table other than Basics (bank 0, which the firmware already has as its four WAVE shapes; 3 presets use that one); they draw on 111 distinct banks (top: 4 basic.tricircle x8, 21 basic.beatpair x5, 5 basic.softhard x4, 26 basic.crushsaw x4, 45 vocal.whisper x3, 77 metallic.fmdeep x3).

## Final selection (170 presets, after the render-figure swap)

Counts: BASS 40, LEAD 20, POLY 20, PAD 10, PLUCK 10, STAB 10, KEYS 10, BRASS 10, FX 10, SEQ 10, TEXTURE 10, BELL 10.

Chosen by wt-score inside each category, cost allowing the category's voices, no two within 0.015 on the 72 knobs; then every candidate was rendered and a preset whose figures (centroid, low/mid/high split, attack, hold) lay within 0.06 of a better one in its category was replaced by the next candidate (13 candidates rejected, 11 presets swapped in). Fit of the final set: mean 82.3, min 59.7, max 100.0; per-voice cost mean 1.20, max 1.71 (5-voice budget: every preset fits its recommended voice count). 7 presets carry a degrade for cost (width, drive, ring, ...).

Wavetables for this set: **56 banks, 56 files, 7.28 MB** on the card (16-bit, <= 32 frames of 2048). 56 presets play a table other than Basics; 3 use the Basics morph (saw, square, triangle, sine: the firmware already has it as its WAVE shapes, but with a continuous position the panel lacks).

## What fits and what does not

The panel's 72 knobs carry the core of a CATHODE patch faithfully: oscillator level/octave/semitone/fine, a PD oscillator with the Saw type, a
sine sub, noise, ring mod, unison (1-4) with detune, 8 filter models, filter 2, HPF, the 7 grit rows, the amp ADSR (up to 2.5 s) and the
Env2 filter sweep, one LFO route, wheel and aftertouch to cutoff. Bass and lead patches built on a PD or saw core with a Classic/Acid/Ladder
filter lose the least. What does not fit is the part the designers wrote for: the whole FX chain (reverb, delay, chorus: in almost every preset), velocity
to cutoff / position / level (the panel has only amp velocity), per-oscillator envelopes, second and third LFO routes, the step modulator, FM, fold,
the resonator, the punch transient, ALGO (Plaits) and Bitmask oscillators, PD types other than Saw, pulse width modulation and glide. Presets whose
character is a wavetable sweep need the tables and a position control. So POLY/PAD material fits worse than BASS because it leans on FX and
motion, and BELL, TEXTURE and STAB carry the most dropped features. Worth bringing: the banks the best-fitting presets actually use (the
`brought` list), every one a procedural bank, 16-bit, 16-32 frames: a few MB on the card and, with the decimated-mip plan, well under 1 MB resident
per table. Basic and Harmonic banks cover most WT oscillators, Vocal/Metallic/Chip/Complex banks supply the unmistakable textures; the
choice is by use in the selection, not by catalogue order. Quality still needs listening: these scores measure what is representable, not what
sounds good.
